## Experiments Performed

### Experiment 1: The Normal Distribution

**Demonstration Link:**  
https://demonstrations.wolfram.com/TheNormalDistribution/

**Description:**  
Studied the Normal Distribution by changing the mean and standard deviation and observing their effects on the probability density curve.

**Parameters Used:**

| Case | Mean (μ) | Standard Deviation (σ) |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0 | 1 |
| 2 | 2 | 1 |
| 3 | 0 | 2 |

**Observations:**

- The standard Normal Distribution has a mean of 0 and a standard deviation of 1.
- Increasing the mean shifts the curve towards the right.
- Increasing the standard deviation makes the curve wider and flatter.
- The mean determines the centre, while the standard deviation determines the spread.

### Experiment 2: The Exponential Distribution

**Demonstration Link:**  
https://demonstrations.wolfram.com/TheExponentialDistribution/

**Description:**  
Studied the Exponential Distribution by changing lambda (λ) and observing its effect on the probability density curve, mean and variance.

**Parameters Used:**

| Case | Lambda (λ) | Mean | Variance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0.5 | 2 | 4 |
| 2 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 3 | 2 | 0.5 | 0.25 |

**Observations:**

- The Exponential Distribution represents waiting times between events in a Poisson process.
- Increasing lambda makes the curve decrease more rapidly.
- The mean is inversely proportional to lambda.
- The variance decreases as lambda increases.
